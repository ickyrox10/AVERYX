Frequently Asked Questions

Open Profile and select Edit Profile. You can update your nickname, email and phone details from there.
Your profile picture uses a subscription tier logo. You can select any tier that you have already unlocked. Locked tier logos cannot be selected.
Use the Forgot Password option from the password recovery page. Follow the recovery instructions to create a new password.
Open Subscription, select the tier you want, review its required deposit and follow the deposit instructions shown for that tier.
A higher tier becomes available when its required deposit level has been reached. Your Profile will automatically recognize the highest unlocked level.
The Fool is the basic starting tier. It requires 0 USDT deposit and displays 0 USDT/day.
Open Wallet → Deposit, select the correct network and use the displayed deposit address. Always verify the network before sending USDT.
The same deposit address may be used for deposits associated with different profiles. Withdrawal-address rules are handled separately.
Always confirm the network before sending USDT. Sending through an unsupported or incorrect network can cause funds to become inaccessible.
Open Wallet → Withdraw, enter your destination address and amount, then review the withdrawal details before confirming.
No. A withdrawal address can only be associated with one profile for withdrawals. The same address cannot be used for withdrawals across multiple profiles.
A pending status means the withdrawal has not reached a completed state yet. Check Records for the latest status and transaction details.
Every account has a unique referral code. A referral reward is recorded when a referred user joins through your referral and completes a qualifying deposit.
The current referral reward is 5% of the qualifying deposit.
Open Refer & Earn to view your referral information. Referral activity and recorded rewards are also shown in Records.
Use a strong password, never share your password or recovery information, and review your account activity regularly.
Open Profile → Change Password. If you do not remember your current password, use the Forgot Password option instead.
Open Records from the bottom navigation. Deposits, withdrawals, subscription and referral activity can be tracked there.
Scroll to the Telegram Support section at the bottom of this page and contact support directly.
An account may be suspended when activity requires a security or compliance review. This can include duplicate account misuse, suspicious activity, abuse of platform features, or other violations of platform rules.
Access to protected account features may be restricted, and you may be logged out while the account remains suspended. Contact support only if you believe the suspension was applied in error.
Do not create additional accounts to bypass a restriction. Attempting to evade an account restriction can lead to further action. Resolve the issue through the official support channel.
Keep your account information accurate, protect your login credentials, avoid abusive or deceptive activity, and do not attempt to bypass security controls or account restrictions.
Support can review reports of an incorrect suspension, but restoration depends on the result of the account review. Please provide accurate account details when requesting assistance.
